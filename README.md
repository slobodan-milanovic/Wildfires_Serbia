# Wildfires_Serbia

Satellite-based reconstructions of wildfire spread in Serbia — fire arrival
time, rate of spread, fire radiative power and released energy — following the
NERO methodology (Benali & Campos, COST Action CA22164).

*Rekonstrukcije širenja šumskih požara u Srbiji iz satelitskih podataka: vreme
dolaska vatre, brzina širenja, snaga zračenja i oslobođena energija, po NERO
metodologiji.*

## Fires · Požari

| Fire · Požar | Period | Site · Sajt |
|---|---|---|
| Deliblato Sands · Deliblatska peščara | 5–28 August 2026 | [deliblato2026](https://slobodan-milanovic.github.io/Wildfires_Serbia/deliblato2026/) |

Each folder is a self-contained static site (SR/EN switch in the top-right
corner) with an interactive fire radiative power page, a fire behaviour
dashboard, spread and FRP animations, and figures. English versions of the
pages, animations and figures carry the `_en` suffix.

*Svaki folder je zaseban statičan sajt (prekidač SR/EN gore desno) sa
interaktivnom FRP stranicom, kontrolnom tablom, animacijama širenja i FRP-a i
grafikonima. Engleske verzije nose sufiks `_en`.*

## Method · Metoda

The reconstruction application was developed on the basis of the methodology
presented by Akli Benali (University of Lisbon) and Marcos Campos (Força
Especial de Proteção Civil) at the NERO Café webinar *Practical tools to
reconstruct wildfire spread* (May 2024, COST Action CA22164 — NERO), and of
the published work of the same group, above all the PT-FireSprd database.

- Benali, A. & Campos, M. (2024). *Practical tools to reconstruct wildfire
  spread.* NERO Café webinar, COST Action CA22164 — NERO. https://nero-network.eu
- Benali, A., Guiomar, N., Gonçalves, H., Mota, B., Silva, F., Fernandes, P. M.,
  Mota, C., Penha, A., Santos, J., Pereira, J. M. C. & Sá, A. C. L. (2023). The
  Portuguese Large Wildfire Spread database (PT-FireSprd). *Earth System Science
  Data*, 15, 3791–3818. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-15-3791-2023

## Data · Podaci

Geospatial results (GeoPackage, time-of-arrival raster, metrics tables) are not
in this repository because of their size; they are archived separately with a
DOI. Sources and attribution are listed in `LICENSE` and on each site page.
